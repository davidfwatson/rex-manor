Hello neighbors,

As the weather cools down and we head into fall, there are plenty of great events and activities happening around Mountain View. I wanted to share a few highlights to keep on your radar over the next few weeks.

### Neighborhood Life

There is a great event coming up right here in our area. Recently on the neighborhood mailing list, Tracy Ferea shared an invitation to the "Going Electric" House Tour & Biodiversity Fundraising Event. It is a wonderful opportunity to see some of the energy-efficient updates our neighbors are making to their homes, while also supporting local biodiversity efforts. Check the Rex Manor Google Group for more details on how to attend.

### Downtown Music and Art

Downtown Mountain View remains vibrant with ongoing outdoor music and new retail spaces.

The **Music on Castro** series continues to bring live performances to the street every Wednesday evening, running through October. Additionally, you can still catch the final **Concerts on the Plaza** events every Friday evening from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. at Civic Center Plaza through the end of September.

While you are downtown, be sure to check out the MOMENT pop-up program, which has been doing a fantastic job activating storefront spaces. Right now, the space at 293 Castro Street is hosting "Don't Eat Me," a collective of local artists. It is a great addition to the downtown mix and a fun spot to discover unique local art.

### Celebrating Local Tech

We are fortunate to live in a city that embraces innovation and growth. The technology industry continues to bring positive energy and economic vitality to Mountain View. If you are looking for an inspiring weekend activity, a visit to the Computer History Museum is always highly recommended. Their exhibits offer a fantastic look at the technological heritage that makes our region so unique.

If you have any other events or updates to share, feel free to post them to the Google Group or email me directly at david@rexmanor.org.

— David Watson
