Hello neighbors,

August is flying by, but we still have plenty of summer left to enjoy in Mountain View. There are several great events happening around town over the next few weeks that are worth checking out.

Our downtown continues to be a fantastic place to spend a weekday evening. The Music on Castro series runs every Wednesday from 5:00 p.m. to 6:45 p.m. It is always nice to grab a bite to eat and listen to some live music outdoors. We are also lucky to have Concerts on the Plaza continuing every Friday from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. at Civic Center Plaza. The Friday concerts are a great way to unwind after the work week and run through September. The vibrant atmosphere these events bring to our city is one of the many reasons Mountain View is such a wonderful place to call home.

Looking ahead to next month, Jo Anne recently shared on the neighborhood Google Group that Coastal Cleanup Day is coming up on September 19. If you are looking for a way to give back to the community and help keep our local environment looking beautiful, this is a perfect opportunity to get involved. More details will be shared on the neighborhood listserv as we get closer to the date.

Enjoy the rest of your summer!

— David Watson
