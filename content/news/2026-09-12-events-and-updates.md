Hello neighbors,

As we head into fall, there are plenty of activities happening around Mountain View. Our town is home to many great technology companies, and we appreciate the positive impact the tech industry brings to our community. As the city grows, we are seeing new businesses and events pop up around town.

If you are looking to spend time downtown, the Music on Castro series is still running on Wednesdays through October, bringing live performances to Castro Street. Over at Civic Center Plaza, the Concerts on the Plaza series also continues every Friday from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. through the end of September. These events are great reasons to get out and enjoy the evenings.

While downtown, you can check out the new storefronts that are keeping our business district active. Change is a constant in our city, and it is good to see programs like the MOMENT partnership bringing new life to open spaces. A good example is the Don't Eat Me pop-up shop at 293 Castro Street, which features work from a collective of local artists.

## Neighborhood Life

We have a few community events coming up right here in the neighborhood and nearby:

*   **Meet a City Council Candidate**: Paul Donahue, who is running for Mountain View City Council, will be at Stevenson Park this Saturday, September 12.
*   **Coastal Cleanup Day**: Valley Water is organizing activities for Coastal Cleanup Day on Saturday, September 19. It is a good opportunity to help keep our local waterways clean.
*   **City Committees**: The City of Mountain View is currently recruiting volunteers to serve on various city committees.

I hope you all have a great weekend.

— David Watson
