Hello neighbors,

Summer is in full swing here in Rex Manor and Mountain View. As we head into July, our city has some great events lined up for us to get out, enjoy the weather, and connect with our community.

To kick things off, the City of Mountain View is hosting its annual Fourth of July Fireworks Spectacular at Shoreline Amphitheatre, featuring a performance by the San Francisco Symphony. It is always a wonderful evening of music and celebration right in our backyard.

Throughout the month, you can also catch the Concerts on the Plaza series. These free concerts happen every Friday evening from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. at Civic Center Plaza. It is a perfect excuse to pack a picnic, grab some lawn chairs, and enjoy live music with friends and neighbors. We are fortunate to live in a growing, dynamic city that continues to add such vibrant public events and amenities, supported by the innovation of our local tech community.

I hope to see many of you out and about at these events!

David Watson
