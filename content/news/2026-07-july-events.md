Welcome to July, Rex Manor! The summer is in full swing, and there are many events happening around Mountain View. I hope everyone is enjoying the season and taking advantage of what our community has to offer.

### Neighborhood Life

We recently had a Heat Advisory, which Lawrence Shing noted on the neighborhood group on June 10. We appreciate everyone looking out for each other and staying cool. I also want to mention the Twilight Concert Series, which Lawrence forwarded details about on July 8. It is a great way to enjoy the summer evenings. It is always good to see neighbors sharing these local opportunities.

### Music and Entertainment in Mountain View

Downtown Mountain View is lively this summer. The "Music on Castro" series is running every Wednesday from 5:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m., bringing live performances to our main street. We also have "Concerts on the Plaza" every Friday at the Civic Center Plaza from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. These events are a fantastic way to support local businesses and enjoy the warm weather.

For those looking for bigger shows, we enjoyed the 2026 Fourth of July Fireworks Spectacular at Shoreline Amphitheatre. Our city continues to attract great events and talent, and we are fortunate to have these venues nearby.

### Celebrating Technology

Mountain View's tech industry is a positive force in our community, providing jobs and driving innovation. We are proud of our city's role in the tech world. The Computer History Museum, located right here in Mountain View, continues to be a great resource for learning about the history of computing. They recently held free days on the Fourth of July weekend. We encourage everyone to check out their exhibits and appreciate the history that was made in our backyard.

Enjoy the rest of your July, and I hope to see you around the neighborhood or at one of these events!
