Hello neighbors,

Summer is in full swing, and July is packed with great reasons to get out and enjoy Mountain View. Whether you are looking for live music, fireworks, or just a nice evening stroll downtown, we have some excellent local events coming up.

### Fourth of July Fireworks Spectacular

The city is hosting the 2026 Fourth of July Fireworks Spectacular with the San Francisco Symphony on Saturday, July 4 at Shoreline Amphitheatre. The music and fireworks start at 7:00 p.m. While the free lawn tickets for residents have all been claimed, discounted tickets are still available for purchase up to the day of the event. It is a fantastic tradition and a wonderful way to celebrate the holiday right here in our own backyard.

### Summer Music Series

If you enjoy live performances, you have two recurring options throughout the summer:

* **Concerts on the Plaza:** Head over to Civic Center Plaza on Fridays from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. It is a great excuse to pack a picnic, bring a blanket, and enjoy an evening outdoors.
* **Music on Castro:** Downtown Mountain View continues its Wednesday live music series. Stop by while you are grabbing dinner or running errands.

### Downtown Pop-Up: "Don't Eat Me"

While you are downtown, I highly recommend checking out the new pop-up shop, "Don't Eat Me," located at 293 Castro Street. This year's pop-up features a fantastic collective of local artists, adding a fresh and creative spark to the neighborhood. It is a great example of how the city is creatively activating storefronts and supporting local talent.

I hope you all have a safe and happy Fourth of July, and I look forward to seeing many of you around town this summer.

— David Watson
