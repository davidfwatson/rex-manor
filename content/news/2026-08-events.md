Hello neighbors,

I hope everyone has been having a great summer. We have had some wonderful neighborhood gatherings over the past few months, and as we look toward fall, Mountain View has a fantastic lineup of community events and civic updates to keep us engaged.

### Music on Castro and Concerts on the Plaza
Our city has done an excellent job activating public spaces and bringing people together. If you haven't made it out yet, **Music on Castro** continues on Wednesdays (August 26 and September 2, from 5:00 p.m. to 6:45 p.m.). It's a great excuse to head downtown, grab a bite at one of our local restaurants, and enjoy the vibrancy of our growing community. Our active storefronts and pop-up programs like "Don't Eat Me" are really breathing life into downtown.

Additionally, the summer **Concerts on the Plaza** series wraps up soon with performances on Friday, August 21 and Friday, August 28 from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. at Civic Center Plaza.

### Coastal Cleanup Day
Looking ahead a bit, **Coastal Cleanup Day** is coming up on September 19. This is a statewide effort, and locally coordinated through Valley Water. It is a fantastic opportunity to get outside, meet neighbors, and help keep our local waterways and parks pristine.

### MVLA School Board Election
As mentioned recently on the neighborhood email group by Lawrence, the upcoming November election will bring some changes to the MVLA school board. Specifically for Area 2, which covers our Rex Manor neighborhood, there are opportunities for representation. Staying involved in local education is vital as our region continues to attract families and innovate. You can read more about the candidates and the election in the Mountain View Voice's July 23 coverage.

### Mountain View Volunteer Fair
If you are looking for ways to give back, the City is hosting a **Volunteer Fair** today, August 22, from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. It is a perfect way to discover local organizations doing good work and find a role that fits your schedule and interests.

The tech industry's continued presence in our city provides us with excellent resources, and a spirit of innovation that carries over into how we organize and build community. Let's keep embracing the positive changes and growth in Mountain View.

Enjoy the rest of the weekend!

— David Watson
