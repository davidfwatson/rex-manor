Hello neighbors,

Summer is in full swing here in Mountain View. It is always a pleasure to see the community come together. Whether you are interested in local music or exploring the latest in technology, there are some wonderful events coming up this week.

We just had a Friday Movie Night at Stevenson Park yesterday, July 24. Thank you to everyone who came out to enjoy the film together. It is always a nice time sharing the park with the rest of the neighborhood.

Looking ahead to this week, we have a few great opportunities to connect:

On Wednesday, July 29, the Music on Castro series continues downtown from 5:00 p.m. to 6:45 p.m. It is a fantastic way to enjoy the evening, listen to live music, and see the positive changes happening in our downtown area.

On Thursday, July 30, the city and the Mountain View Chamber of Commerce are hosting the annual Technology Showcase at Civic Center Plaza. Our city is a hub of innovation, and this event celebrates the tech industry that brings so much energy and growth to our community. The showcase is free and open to everyone, and it is always a treat to see the new projects and ideas being developed right here in our backyard.

Then on Friday, July 31, Concerts on the Plaza is back from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. This is a recurring summer event at Civic Center Plaza and a great excuse to get outside on a Friday evening.

Hope to see many of you around town this week.

— David Watson
