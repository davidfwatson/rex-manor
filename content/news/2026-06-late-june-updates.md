Hello neighbors,

Summer is officially in full swing, and there are some great events happening around Mountain View over the next couple of weeks to enjoy the long days. We also have a few updates from around the neighborhood.

### TechFest: Maker’s Lab

For those who love to tinker or just want to see some incredible local innovation, the Computer History Museum is hosting **TechFest: Maker’s Lab** today, Saturday, June 20. It is a fantastic event exploring how inventors, makers, and artists use technology to expand creativity. Our local tech scene is a huge part of what makes Mountain View so special, and events like this are a fun, hands-on way to celebrate that spirit. Admission includes full access to the museum, so it is a great excuse to check out their exhibits, too!

### Concerts on the Plaza

A reminder that the city's wonderful **Concerts on the Plaza** series is back for the summer! Every Friday from June through September, you can head down to Civic Center Plaza from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. to enjoy free live music. It is always a relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere, so grab a blanket and maybe grab dinner downtown beforehand.
