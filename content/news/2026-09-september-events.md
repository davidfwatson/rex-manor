Hi neighbors,

As we transition into late summer, September brings some great opportunities to connect with our community and get involved in local activities.

### Coastal Cleanup Day

On Saturday, September 19, from 9:00 a.m. to noon, the Creek Connections Action Group is coordinating Coastal Cleanup Day across Santa Clara County. This is a chance to help remove litter and debris from our local creeks and natural areas. Preventing trash from reaching the waterways is a great way to protect local wildlife and keep our neighborhoods clean. If you want to participate, you can volunteer at a site or even coordinate a cleanup yourself. Supplies like gloves and trash bags are provided by the organizers.

### Community Emergency Response Team Training

The City of Mountain View is offering a CERT Academy this fall for those interested in emergency preparedness. Classes run on Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 6:30 to 9:30 p.m. at the Auditorium on Villa Street, starting Thursday, September 10, and finishing with a Field Day class on September 26. The training is free and covers valuable skills to help our neighborhood during emergencies like earthquakes.

In addition, there is a County-wide CERT Exercise scheduled for Saturday, September 12, at Mission College. It involves teams responding to simulated scenarios to practice skills in real time.

### Concerts on the Plaza

The summer music series continues! Concerts on the Plaza is running every Friday night through September from 6:00 to 7:30 p.m. at Civic Center Plaza in Downtown Mountain View. Grab some food, bring your friends, and enjoy the live music. The new Plaza Green outdoor space is also open and features seasonal games and seating to relax in. It is wonderful to see the downtown area energized and continuing to provide gathering spaces for all residents.

Enjoy the rest of your August and hope to see you at some of these events!

Best,
David Watson
