Hello neighbors,

September is here and the weather is starting to cool down. I wanted to share a few events happening around Mountain View this month that might be of interest to Rex Manor residents.

If you enjoy live music, the Concerts on the Plaza series is wrapping up its summer run. These free concerts happen every Friday night from 6:00 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. at Civic Center Plaza in downtown Mountain View. It is a good opportunity to bring some lawn chairs, pick up dinner from a local restaurant, and spend time with neighbors.

For those interested in technology, the Computer History Museum (CHM) has a couple of notable events. The "Apple at 50" exhibit, which features rare prototypes of classic Apple products, is closing on September 7. Later in the month, on Wednesday, September 23 at 7:00 p.m., CHM Live is hosting "In Conversation with Boris Cherny, Creator of Claude Code." The tech industry is a big part of what makes our city thrive, and having these resources so close by is a great benefit.

I also noticed on our neighborhood Google Group that some neighbors have been sharing information about local city council candidate meet-and-greets. Be sure to check the rex-manor-neighbors group for those details if you want to get involved and learn more about local issues.

If you have any neighborhood announcements for future updates, feel free to email me at david@rexmanor.org.

— David Watson
