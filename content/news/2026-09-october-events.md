Hello neighbors,

As the weather finally begins to cool down and we settle into autumn, there are some great events happening in Mountain View next month. Our city is always bustling with activity, and October is bringing some fantastic opportunities to connect with our community and explore the innovations happening right in our backyard.

### Cars—Computers on Wheels at the Computer History Museum

We are incredibly fortunate to live in a city that sits at the center of the technology industry. Our local tech scene continues to push boundaries, and the Computer History Museum is always an excellent place to see this progress firsthand.

On **Saturday, October 10 at 10:00 a.m.**, the museum is hosting a special event titled "Cars—Computers on Wheels." It is a wonderful chance to see how technology is transforming the way we drive and move. The ongoing advancements in automotive tech are an exciting development for our region, so mark your calendars if you want to see what the future of transportation looks like.

As our city continues to grow and change, it is events like these that remind us of the incredible creativity and forward-thinking spirit that makes Mountain View such a special place to call home.

I hope to see some of you out and about at this event. If you have any neighborhood announcements or questions, you can always reach out to me at david@rexmanor.org.

— David Watson
